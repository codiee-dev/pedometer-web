Walk further, every day.

Walker counts your steps with the motion sensors already inside your iPhone, keeps your streak alive, and records walks, runs and rides with GPS. No watch, no account.

What's inside

  • A daily step goal with a live ring, distance, calories and floors
  • Streaks, with streak freezes for the days you miss
  • GPS workouts with route map, splits and pace
  • Week, month and year progress, records and over 100 medals
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

Help

Steps not counting, a question about Pro, or a refund? The support page has the answers, and an address to write to if it doesn't.
